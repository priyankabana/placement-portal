------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  NOTES
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  * A student registered later from the portal gets the same kind of password
    automatically (StudentService.create -> AuthService.defaultPasswordFor).
  * A student can apply to an open drive from their own profile page. The CGPA
    cut off, the backlog limit and the duplicate check still apply.
  * A student cannot open the officer screens - config/AuthInterceptor.java
    matches the allowed URLs with a pattern and sends them back to /my-profile.
  * Passwords are plain text on purpose, this list is handed to the students.
    Hashing with BCrypt is listed as future scope.
